Audio folder
============

Drop .ogg / .wav / .mp3 files into these folders and they'll auto-load at game start.
The filename (without extension) is the key passed to AudioManager.

Currently wired SFX keys:
  pump      — played when the handcar lever is pumped
  harvest   — played when hitting a harvestable
  chop      — played when chopping a tree (wood)
  metal_hit — played when hitting metal scrap
  place     — played when a building is placed

Currently wired ambient keys:
  forest  — looping background in forest biome
  desert  — looping background in desert biome
  snow    — looping background in snow biome
  plains  — looping background in plains biome
  night   — looping background at night
